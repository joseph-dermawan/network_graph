## 🧭 3. Methodological Audit Checklist for CapEx Analysis
1. **Gross vs. Net CapEx**: Always verify whether reported CapEx includes proceeds from asset sales or landlord reimbursements.
2. **Lease Obligations**: In addition to Cash Statement CapEx, audit **Equipment acquired under finance/capital leases** and off-balance sheet commitments.
3. **Depreciation Life Distortion**: Quantify the earnings lift generated by extending server depreciation schedules from 3-4 years to 5-6 years. A 1-year extension on a $100B server asset base reduces annual depreciation expense by ~$5B-$8B, artificially inflating GAAP operating margins!
4. **Customer Concentration Interlock**: Cross-reference Hyperscaler CapEx spending line items with Nvidia/TSMC/Broadcom Data Center revenue to measure the **CapEx Payout Ratio (CPR)**.
