## 🎯 Executive Principles & Hypotheses
1. **The Separation of Builders vs. Buyers**: Builders (NVIDIA, TSMC, Broadcom) are capturing upfront cash while Hyperscalers (Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta) are capitalizing hundreds of billions into depreciating assets.
2. **The Depreciation Delay**: Current reported GAAP operating profits are buffered by extended server accounting schedules (5–6 years), masking the underlying 3.5-year GPU replacement cadence.
3. **The Duration Limit**: Big Tech's operating cash flows can self-fund the buildout for approximately **18 to 24 months (until H1 2027)** before margin compression and buyback constraints trigger a capital expenditure pause.
4. **The Transmission Line**: When the pause hits, the pain will transmit primarily to hardware suppliers via the economic **Accelerator Principle**, mirroring Cisco's 2001 trajectory.
