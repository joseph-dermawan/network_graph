## 2. Company Overview & Business Segment Architecture

Rolls-Royce operates through three mission-critical divisions commanding structural oligopolies:

```

│                              ROLLS-ROYCE HOLDINGS PLC                                  │

│ - Trent XWB, 1000, 7000  │   Propulsion (AUKUS)     │ - #1 Hyperscale AI DC Gensets    │
│ - TotalCare LTSA Model   │ - EJ200 (Eurofighter)    │ - Microgrids & Battery Storage   │

└──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────┘
```

### A. Civil Aerospace (The Cash Generation Engine)
* **The "Razor-and-Blade" Model:** Engines are delivered to airframers (Airbus A350, A330neo, Boeing 787) near cost, binding airlines to 10-to-20-year Long-Term Service Agreements (LTSAs / TotalCare). 
* **Flying Hour Economics:** Revenue and cash flow are indexed to Engine Flying Hours (EFH). Global widebody flying hours have fully recovered beyond 105% of 2019 levels.
* **Pricing Power:** Erginbilgic’s renegotiation campaign eliminated loss-making "time and materials" caps, ensuring that inflation in high-pressure turbine blade metallurgy is 100% passed through to airline customers.

### B. Defence (The Sovereign Monopoly)
* **Nuclear Submarine Sole Provider:** Rolls-Royce Submarines is the exclusive designer and manufacturer of nuclear steam raising plants for the Royal Navy (Vanguard, Astute, and the upcoming Dreadnought ballistic missile class), reinforced by the historic AUKUS trilateral submarine alliance.
* **Combat Aviation:** Powers the Eurofighter Typhoon (EJ200) and won the sole engine contract for the US Army's Future Long-Range Assault Aircraft (FLRAA) via the Bell V-280 Valor.
* **Revenue Visibility:** Government-guaranteed cost-plus and incentive fee structures create an impervious revenue floor.

### C. Power Systems (The AI Data Center Play)
* **mtu Brand Leadership:** Global leader in ultra-reliable standby power generation engines.
* **Hyperscale AI Tailwind:** A modern 100MW–500MW AI data center requires massive redundant diesel/gas gensets to guarantee 99.999% uptime. Rolls-Royce Power Systems has captured top-tier market share among Tier 1 hyperscalers (Microsoft, AWS, Google), with order backlogs extending through 2028.

### D. Rolls-Royce SMR (Small Modular Reactors)
* Leading the UK consortium for factory-built 470MWe modular nuclear reactors. Shortlisted by Great British Nuclear (GBN) and CEZ in the Czech Republic. Represents an unmodeled free call option on European nuclear decarbonization.
