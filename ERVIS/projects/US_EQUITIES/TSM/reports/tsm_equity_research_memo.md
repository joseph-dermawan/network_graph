TSMC operates a pure-play foundry model, providing advanced wafer manufacturing, lithography, masking, and advanced packaging services to over 500 fabless semiconductor and systems customers.

### The Three Pillars of TSMC's Physical Monopoly:
1. **The Advanced Packaging Bottleneck (CoWoS):** The generative AI compute race is no longer constrained solely by silicon wafer yields; it is constrained by 2.5D advanced packaging. TSMC's **Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate (CoWoS)** integrates logic dies with High Bandwidth Memory (HBM3e) using sub-micron silicon interposers. TSMC's aggressive expansion of Advanced Packaging Fab 6 (AP6) and the new Chiayi AP7 facility will scale capacity from ~35,000 wafers per month (wpm) in 2024 to >75,000 wpm by late 2026, monopolizing 100% of the packaging tier for NVIDIA and AMD.
2. **ASML EUV Tool Fleet & GigaFab Scale:** TSMC operates the world's largest fleet of ASML Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) lithography machines (>120 EUV steppers), outstripping Intel and Samsung combined. Its GigaFab clusters in Hsinchu (Fab 12), Central Taiwan Science Park (Fab 15), and Tainan (Fab 18) achieve unparalleled operational yield efficiencies through 24/7 autonomous material transport systems (AMHS) and cross-fab operational learning curves.
3. **Leading-Edge Node Cadence (N3 & N2):** While competitor foundries (Samsung Foundry, Intel Foundry Services) have struggled with gate-all-around (GAA) yield degradation and customer cancellations, TSMC successfully ramped 3nm (N3B, N3E, N3P) to full capacity. Its upcoming 2nm (N2) nanosheet GAA architecture with backside power delivery (Super Power Rail / A16) enters commercial volume production in 2025/2026 with 100% pre-booked allocations from Apple, NVIDIA, and AMD.
