2. Industry Analysis
Market Dynamics
Structure-Consolidated at the top but fragmented at the bottom, the global
switchgear market is highly competitive, with the top 5 companies (ABB,
Siemens, Schneider Electric, Eaton and Hitachi Energy) taking up 30% of the
medium-voltage switchgear market, with the remainder divided among regional
electrical companies
Growth–the underlying switchgear industry is not a hyper-growth industry by
itself. Current estimates place the global medium-voltage switchgear market at
approximately US$46–57B in 2025, with projected growth of roughly 5.7–6.5%
CAGR through 2030–35. However, while traditional sectors are expected to grow
moderately, specialised areas including AI infrastructure and data centres are
growing rapidly.
