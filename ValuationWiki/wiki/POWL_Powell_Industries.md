# Powell Industries (NASDAQ: POWL)

**Powell Industries, Inc.** (NASDAQ: POWL) is a leading custom manufacturer of complex, high-specification electrical power distribution equipment, custom Power Control Rooms (PCRs), switchgear, motor control centers, and integrated electrical automation systems. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, Powell serves critical energy infrastructure, hyperscale AI data centers, electric utilities, and heavy industrial facilities.

## 🏭 Operational Infrastructure & Competitive Advantage

### 1. Custom Power Control Rooms (PCRs) & System Integration
Unlike commoditized off-the-shelf component suppliers, Powell specializes in pre-fabricated, fully integrated **Power Control Rooms (PCRs)**. These complex enclosures house custom medium-voltage switchgear, circuit breakers, and automated power distribution units tailored for extreme thermal loads in hyperscale AI data centers and offshore LNG terminals.

### 2. Jacintoport Expansion (+62% Productive Yard Space)
To execute against its record $2.4B backlog, Powell completed a **$12.4M capacity expansion** at its Jacintoport manufacturing facility in Houston, adding approximately **335,000 square feet** (+62% productive yard space). This expansion directly increases physical manufacturing throughput, allowing backlog burn rates to sustain high levels.

### 3. High Fixed-Cost Operating Leverage
Powell's cost structure relies heavily on skilled technician labor and fixed fabrication facility overhead across Houston hubs. Consequently, incremental revenue gains produce powerful **volume leverage**:
* **Q1 FY2026 YoY Performance:** Revenue grew +4% YoY to $312M, while Gross Profit jumped **+20% YoY** to $87M.
* **Margin Expansion:** Gross Margins expanded from **25.0% to 28.0%**, with management explicitly attributing the gains to favorable volume leverage and factory throughput.
