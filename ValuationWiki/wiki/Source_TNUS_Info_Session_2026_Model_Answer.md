## 🎯 Problem Statement & Scope

* **Area of Focus:** Identify a publicly listed company that is **NOT an AI model developer or semiconductor manufacturer**, but stands to **benefit disproportionately** from the global scaling of AI infrastructure.
* **Universe:** Globally listed company with a minimum market capitalization of **US$1.0 Billion**.
* **Recommendation & Horizon:** Long/Buy recommendation based on a **3 to 5-year investment horizon**.
* **Deliverables:** Main report capped at 10 pages (unlimited appendix) + attached financial model. Finalists pitch 15 slides (10 min pitch + 10 min Q&A).

## 📊 Complete Grading Rubrics (100% Base + 5% Bonus)

| Criteria | Max Weight | Key Evaluated Elements & Expectations |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Financial Analysis** | **25%** | Deep understanding of profitability, liquidity, growth forecasts, past performance vs. projected outcomes. Data-driven insights tied directly to investment theses. |
| **Investment Theses** | **20%** | Heart of the pitch! Forward-looking, data-supported arguments articulating a clear **variant view** that challenges status quo market consensus. |
| **Valuation** | **20%** | Rigor of DCF, price multiples, SOTP (if multi-segment), justification of assumptions, coherence and alignment with investment theses. |
| **Industry & Competitive Positioning** | **10%** | Broader industry trends, market dynamics, competitive forces (Porter's 5 Forces, moats) linked to future growth potential. |
| **Executive Summary** | **5%** | High-impact initial hook capturing pitch essence, key financial insights, and strategic thesis. |
| **Company Analysis** | **5%** | Deep business model understanding, revenue drivers, segment breakdown, management team execution. |
| **Risk & Catalysts** | **5%** | Identification of downside risks with concrete mitigations, plus near-term growth catalysts. |
| **Formatting & Flow** | **5%** | Professional, visually appealing design, logical flow, consistent visual aids. |
| **ESG Integration** | **5%** | Strategic, analytical integration of ESG factors into financial performance, risk, and long-term valuation (beyond fluff). |
| **BONUS POINTS** | **+5%** | Awarded for incorporating alternative data, regression models, or stochastic simulations (e.g. Monte Carlo). |

## 💡 The "Model Answer" Pitch Philosophy: To Do vs. To Avoid

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                           THE VARIANT VIEW CRUX                         │
│                                                                         │
│  Sell-Side Consensus View (Priced-In) ──► Market Price                  │
│                                                 ▲                       │
│                                                 │ Disconnect / Re-Rating │
│                                                 ▼                       │
│  Your Variant View (Un-Priced Thesis) ──► Intrinsic Value ($286.00)     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Formulate a Variant View
* **DO:** Communicate a unique, non-consensus perspective. Ask: *"What is the market currently missing or mispricing?"*
* **AVOID:** Rehashing sell-side broker reports or repeating company PR releases.

### 2. Identify Needle-Moving Drivers
* **DO:** Pinpoint specific revenue and margin levers (e.g., volume leverage, Jacintoport yard expansion, backlog conversion timing).
* **AVOID:** Getting bogged down in minor operational details that do not shift intrinsic valuation.

### 3. Balance 3-5 Year Horizon with Near-Term Guideposts
* **DO:** Focus on structural 3-5 year macro trends (AI power density surge), but provide near-term guideposts (book-to-bill ratio, quarterly backlog conversion) so investors know when upside will materialize.
* **AVOID:** Ignoring near-term quarterly execution issues, which determine whether market sentiment comes around to your thesis.

### 4. Multiple Expansion Mechanics
* **DO:** Demonstrate *why* perception will shift, leading to valuation multiple re-rating (e.g. from 12x industrial average to 18x AI infrastructure peer multiple).
* **AVOID:** Assuming static historical multiples when business fundamentals are undergoing structural acceleration.
