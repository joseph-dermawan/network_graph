### The Three Transmission Vectors of Google's Margin Dilution:
1. **The CoreWeave Debt Toll:** CoreWeave’s $10.5B debt facility carries an effective coupon of 11.5% to 13.0% (SOFR + 750 bps). To meet debt service, CoreWeave prices GPU cluster rentals at a markup over straight-line depreciation. When Google leases capacity from CoreWeave to hedge cluster shortfalls, **Google effectively pays CoreWeave’s interest expense to Blackstone**, transferring enterprise margin out of Mountain View and into private credit.
2. **Duplicate Silicon R&D Overhead:** Amazon unified its customer compute around internal Trainium 2 and direct owned DGX SuperPODs. Google simultaneously funded three capital sinks:
   - Paying Broadcom for TPU v5p/v6 (Trillium) custom silicon.
   - Purchasing owned H100/H200 clusters directly from Nvidia.
   - Contracting $12.5B in take-or-pay capacity from CoreWeave.
3. **Lease Capitalization Under ASC 842:** Google's multi-year contracts with CoreWeave created billions in operating lease liabilities, inflating technical infrastructure assets while depressing return on invested capital (ROIC).
