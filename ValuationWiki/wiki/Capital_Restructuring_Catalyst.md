# Capital Restructuring & Leveraged Recapitalization Catalyst

This analysis evaluates the accretive impact of a **Strategic Capital Structure Restructuring** on Powell Industries' cost of capital (WACC) and intrinsic equity target price.

---

## 🏛️ Status Quo vs. Restructured Capital Structure

Currently, Powell operates with an unleveraged, 100% equity-heavy balance sheet ($633.56M cash, $2.52M debt). While highly safe, this capital structure leaves zero debt tax shields and results in an elevated Cost of Capital (**WACC = 11.02%**).

### Leveraged Recapitalization Scenario (20% Target Debt Ratio):
* **Debt Issuance Amount:** Powell issues **$1.60 Billion** in investment-grade senior notes at a pre-tax interest rate of **5.50%**.
* **Use of Proceeds:** The entire $1.60B is deployed into a massive **share repurchase program** at an average buyback price of $230/share, retiring approximately **6.96 million shares** (~19.0% of outstanding equity).
* **New Debt / Equity Mix:** 20.0% Debt / 80.0% Equity.

---

## 💡 Strategic Takeaway
By introducing a conservative **20% debt target ratio**, Powell reduces its WACC from **11.02% to 9.79%** through interest tax shield optimization, unlocking an additional **+$34.50 per share** in value and elevating the Target Price to **$320.00+**.
